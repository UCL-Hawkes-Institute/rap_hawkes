# 7. RAP for Medical Imaging Research
23rd July 2026

Our July session was led by Sophie & Lawrence from the Hawkes Institute who covered how to utilise BIDS-tools to efficiently handle neuroimaging data and using DataLad when managing large datasets. The second half of the session also looked at tracking machine learning experimentation with Weights & Biases.
